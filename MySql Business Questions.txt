MySQL Business Questions

🟢 Basic — 5 Questions

1. What is the total revenue, total cost, and gross profit generated from all orders?
2. Which product categories generate the highest sales and total quantity sold?
3. Which regions generate the highest sales, orders, and gross profit?
4. Who are the top 10 customers based on total sales, and how many orders has each customer placed?
5. What is the total sales, amount paid, and outstanding amount by payment status?

🟡 Intermediate — 4 Questions

6. Which products generate high sales but comparatively low profit margins?
7. Which customers have both high sales and high outstanding payments?
8. Which products are currently below their reorder level, and how many months have they experienced low-stock conditions?
9. Which product categories have the longest average production time and what is their average production completion percentage?

🔴 Advanced — 4 Questions

10. Are longer production times associated with higher dispatch delays across product categories
11. How is monthly sales performance changing, and what is the month-over-month sales growth?
12. How concentrated is company revenue among customers, and which customers contribute to the majority of total revenue?
13. Which salespersons generate strong sales and profitability, considering total orders, total sales, gross profit, profit margin, and average order value?
